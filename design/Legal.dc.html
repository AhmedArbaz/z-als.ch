Privacy

Last updated 4 October 2026

This notice describes what happens to the details you give us through this website. It is written to match what the site actually does. If the site changes, this page changes with it.

Who is responsible

Zurich-Airport Limousinen Services GmbH, Am Balsberg 36, 8302 Kloten, Switzerland · info@z-als.ch · {{ dispatchPhone }}

What you choose to send

There is no account, login or profile. When you request a quote or book a ride, the booking form collects:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • The journey: pick-up and destination, date and time, flight number, passengers, luggage, vehicle class and the fare shown.
  • Anything you write in the free-text notes field.

The contact form sends your name, email address, phone number (if you give one), subject and message to our dispatch by email. The website keeps no copy.

Why we hold it

To answer your enquiry, confirm your booking and carry out the transport you ask for: steps taken at your request before a contract, and the performance of that contract. We do not use your details for marketing, and we do not sell or share them for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Resend (Resend, Inc., USA) delivers the booking emails: a copy of your booking to our dispatch, and a confirmation to the email address you give. It also delivers the messages you send with the contact form, to our dispatch only.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd). If you choose to book via WhatsApp, the site opens a chat prefilled with your booking; it is sent only if you tap Send.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd). If you pay online by card or TWINT, you pay on Stripe’s own checkout page. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach our server; we receive the payment status and amount.
  • Mapbox (Mapbox, Inc., USA). While you type a pick-up or destination address, the letters you type are sent through our server to Mapbox to suggest matching addresses. When a trip neither starts nor ends at Zürich Airport, the pick-up and destination are also sent to Mapbox to measure the road distance for the fare. Your IP address is not passed on.
  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd; Google LLC, USA). Only if you accept analytics in the banner, we measure which pages are visited, how visitors reach the site and how far they get in the booking form, to improve the site. Google then receives your IP address and browser details and sets the cookies _ga and _ga_*. The events we send never contain your name, contact details or the text you type. Before you choose, and if you decline, no analytics cookies are set; because the Tag Manager script itself loads from Google, Google’s servers still see your IP address, as with any embedded resource.
  • Unsplash (Unsplash Inc., Canada). Some photographs load from its servers, which therefore receive your IP address and browser details, as with any website you visit. The typefaces are served from this site.

Some of these providers process data outside Switzerland, including in the United States, under their contractual data-protection safeguards.

Cookies and analytics

The site sets no advertising or marketing cookies. The analytics cookies _ga and _ga_* (up to two years) are set only after you accept analytics in the banner, and you can change your mind at any time with “Cookie settings” in the page footer. It keeps three things in your own browser: your language choice (zals-lang), so the site opens in the same language next time; your analytics choice (zals-consent, kept for 12 months); and, only while you pay online, your booking in this tab’s session storage, so the form can show it again when you return from Stripe. Closing the tab clears the last one.

How long we keep it

Booking emails and messages are kept as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting booking. Records that become part of a booking are kept for as long as Swiss accounting law requires. Our web server keeps no copy of the form. Analytics data is kept in Google Analytics for the period set there, at most 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can withdraw your analytics consent at any time with “Cookie settings” in the page footer. Write to info@z-als.ch and we will respond. In Switzerland you may complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU or EEA, to your national supervisory authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS only. Card and TWINT payments are handled entirely by Stripe.

1. Who these terms are with

These terms govern transport services provided by Zurich-Airport Limousinen Services GmbH, Am Balsberg 36, 8302 Kloten, Switzerland.

2. Quotes and bookings

Trips from or to Zürich Airport, and between any two addresses up to 320 km by road, are shown as fixed fares in the booking form. Longer routes, and hourly hire, are shown as estimates and confirmed personally by our dispatch. A booking paid online by card or TWINT is confirmed once the payment succeeds. A booking made via WhatsApp, or to be paid to the chauffeur, is confirmed when our dispatch confirms it to you.

4. What the fare includes

  • Waiting, luggage handling, tolls and the Swiss motorway vignette.
  • Child seats, fitted before the journey, at no charge.
  • Sixty minutes of waiting after landing on airport arrivals.

Nothing in this list is added to the invoice afterwards.

5. Flight monitoring and delays

Where you give us a flight number, we track the inbound flight and move the pick-up time with it at no charge. Waiting beyond the included sixty minutes is charged pro rata at the hourly rate for the class booked, and only after we have contacted you.

6. Changes and cancellation

To change or cancel a booking, contact our dispatch by phone, WhatsApp or email as early as possible. Any cancellation charge is stated when your booking is confirmed.

7. Payment

You can pay online by card or TWINT through Stripe, or pay the chauffeur on the day. Corporate and contract clients are invoiced monthly, with agreed payment terms.

8. Passengers, luggage and conduct

Vehicles are booked by class, not by registration; we supply the stated model or an equivalent. The chauffeur may decline a load that exceeds the vehicle’s safe capacity. Pets and bulky sports luggage are carried where notified in advance.

9. Liability

Passengers are covered by the passenger insurance included in every fare. We are not liable for consequential loss arising from delays outside our control, including weather, road closures and air-traffic disruption.

10. Governing law

Swiss law applies. The place of jurisdiction is Kloten, Switzerland.
